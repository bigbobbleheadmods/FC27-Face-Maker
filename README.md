## 🤔 What is it?

**FC27 Face Maker** is a desktop app that builds a full FC 27 head from a photo of a face. It shapes the head, paints the skin, picks the hair and beard, and exports a face folder that the **FIFA Editor Tool** imports.

Everything runs on your own PC. Your photo never leaves it.

---

## 🎉 What's in v1.0

> The first release. 🥳

- 📷 **Photo to FC 27 head.** Finds the face (478 points) and shapes the FC 27 head to match it automatically.
- 🎨 **Skin matching.** The face texture is painted from your photo and blended into the FC 27 skin tone. Teeth and tongue keep their FC 27 colours.
- 💇 **Hair.** The closest FC 27 hair is picked for you. Browse the **Short**, **Medium** and **Long** tabs and set the colour with the game's own hair colour picker.
- 🧔 **Beard detection.** Facial hair is detected and the closest FC 27 beard is suggested.
- 🧢 **Scalp fit.** The head is fitted to the hair you choose, so there are no gaps or poke-through.
- 🖼️ **Extra reference photos.** Add more photos of the same person to improve the shape.
- 🎚️ **Simple and Advanced shape editors.** Quick controls, or 66 control points plus ear and flesh depth controls.
- 😀 **Pose preview.** Open the mouth, smile, blink or turn the head to check your face. Export always uses the neutral face.
- 🚻 **Male / Female base.** One switch moves the head onto a female base and updates the preview live.
- 👓 **Glasses warning.** You get a heads-up when glasses could spoil the eyes and skin.

## 📸 Tips for the best result

- Use a front-facing photo, looking straight at the camera.
- Use even, soft lighting with no harsh shadows.
- No glasses, and keep the hair off the face if you can.
- Bigger, sharper photos give better skin detail.
